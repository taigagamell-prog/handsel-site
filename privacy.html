Privacy

Two different groups of people give us data: prospective clients who fill in the form on this site, and employees who answer a commuting survey. The rules are different for each, so they are set out separately.

Before publishing: replace the bracketed placeholders below with the registered entity name, registered address and, if applicable, the state of incorporation. Have this reviewed by counsel before a first client engagement. This is a drafted notice, not legal advice.

Who we are

Handsel is a trading name of [LEGAL ENTITY NAME], [REGISTERED ADDRESS]. For questions about this notice or about data we hold, contact hello@handseldata.com.

Part one: this website

What we collect

If you submit the contact form, we collect the name, work email address, company and any message you choose to give us. Nothing else on this site collects personal data.

We do not run analytics, advertising or third party tracking on this site, and we do not set cookies.

How it is processed

Form submissions are delivered to us by Formspree, which processes the submission and forwards it to our email. Their handling is governed by their own privacy policy. Once delivered, submissions live in our Google Workspace mailbox.

Why

To reply to you and, if you ask for it, to send the sample report pack. We do not add you to a marketing list, and we do not sell or share your details with anyone.

How long

Enquiries that do not lead to an engagement are deleted after twenty four months. Enquiries that do become engagements are kept under the client file retention below.

Part two: commuting surveys

This part applies if your employer has engaged Handsel and you have been asked to complete a commuting survey. You are shown this notice before you answer any question.

What we collect

  • Your primary and secondary commuting mode.
  • Your one-way commuting distance, as you estimate it.
  • How many days in a typical week you commute, and how many you work from home.
  • Vehicle fuel type, only if your primary mode is a private vehicle.
  • Your worksite, and in some engagements a broad employment category.

What we do not collect

  • Your home address. Distance is what you tell us, not something we derive from where you live.
  • Your location, at any time. There is no app, no tracking and no background collection.
  • Anything about your journey outside the commute.
  • Special category or sensitive personal information of any kind.

What your employer sees

Aggregates of ten or more

Your employer never sees your individual response, and never sees a figure covering fewer than ten respondents. Where a site or group falls below that floor, it is merged into a larger group or left out of the reporting entirely. Your employer receives totals and averages. They do not receive a row that is you.

Whether you have to answer

Answering is voluntary. If you would rather not, you do not have to give a reason, and your response or lack of one is not reported to your employer at an individual level.

How long

Individual survey responses are retained for the period required to complete the engagement and support any assurance review of it, and are deleted no later than twenty four months after the report is delivered. Aggregated figures, which are not personal data, are retained with the client file.

Who else sees the data

We use a small number of service providers to run the business: Formspree for the website form, Google Workspace for email and file storage, and Vercel for hosting this site. Where an engagement is subject to third party assurance, an assurance provider may need to inspect the underlying evidence file under a confidentiality agreement.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross context behavioural advertising.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what personal information we hold about you, to have it corrected, to have it deleted, and to be free from discrimination for exercising those rights. California residents have these rights under the CCPA as amended by the CPRA.

To exercise any of them, email hello@handseldata.com. We will respond within the period the applicable law requires. We may need to verify your identity first, and for survey data we may need enough information to locate your response.

Security

Data is held in access controlled cloud services with multi factor authentication required on every account that can reach it. Access is limited to those working on the engagement.

Changes

If this notice changes materially, the date at the top changes and the previous version is retained. Survey respondents are shown the version in force at the time they answer.
