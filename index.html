The year you have to report on is the year you are in.

Commuting is the one Scope 3 category you cannot buy your way out of after the fact. There is no invoice trail and no supplier to ask. It is measured by surveying the people who did the commuting, and they forget.

If California's first Scope 3 reports land in 2027 and cover the prior fiscal year, the data in them is being generated in your offices right now. A survey run in mid 2027 asking staff to recall 2026 travel patterns is not evidence. It is a guess with a response rate attached.

This is the whole argument for moving early. Not that the rules are settled, but that the measurement window closes whether they settle or not.

How it runs

Scope call. We agree the reporting boundary, the sites in scope, and which of your existing HR and access data can carry part of the load.

Instrument and field. A short commuting survey, designed for response rate rather than completeness, fielded to your staff over roughly two weeks.

Model and extrapolate. Responses are modelled to the full population with the method stated, the uncertainty stated, and the non-response treatment written down rather than buried.

Deliver and defend. You get the report pack and the workbook. When your assurance provider comes back with questions, we answer them.

Where the rules actually stand

Anyone selling you certainty here is selling you something. As of August 2026 the position is this:

  • California SB 253 is law, and CARB has adopted initial regulations covering Scope 1 and 2 reporting.
  • Scope 3 is still pre-rulemaking. CARB has published options and taken comment but has not adopted a final Scope 3 rule.
  • Three phase-in approaches are on the table. One requires all relevant categories from the start. One phases in by sector. One limits year one to five categories: purchased goods and services, fuel and energy-related activities, waste, business travel, and employee commuting.
  • Under two of those three options, Category 7 is in scope from the first reporting year.

We will tell you when that changes, including if it changes in a direction that means you need us less.
